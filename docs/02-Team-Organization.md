## Team Charter

Team 102 discussed the goals, responsibilities, and working expectations needed to complete Project Aurora successfully. Based on this discussion and the detailed planning recorded in the [Appendix: Team Organization](Appendix/App-Team-Org.md), the team agreed on the following charter:

> Team 102 commits to designing and building Project Aurora through respectful collaboration, dependable communication, shared responsibility, and evidence-based engineering decisions. Each member will complete assigned work on time, attend and participate in team meetings, document their progress, share technical knowledge, and help resolve problems constructively. The team will hold every member accountable while ensuring that all four individual subsystems operate safely and successfully as one complete system.

This charter gives the team a shared standard for decision-making and accountability. The team’s success will be measured not only by completing the course requirements, but also by producing a functional prototype, developing practical engineering skills, and creating professional documentation that each member can include in their portfolio.

## Product Mission Statement

Team 102 considered the intended users, operating environment, primary function, and common failures associated with small irrigation systems. Based on this discussion, the team developed the following product mission statement:

> Project Aurora’s mission is to automatically irrigate a small garden using collected rainwater while conserving water and protecting the pump through soil-moisture, tank-level, and water-flow monitoring.

Project Aurora will activate irrigation only when the soil requires water and the storage tank contains enough water. The system will monitor actual water flow, stop the pump when unsafe operating conditions are detected, and provide clear status or fault indications to the user.

The project will be divided into four modular subsystems: soil-moisture sensing, tank-level sensing, water-flow monitoring, and pump control with user warnings. These subsystems will communicate with one another to create a reliable and practical tabletop irrigation prototype.
