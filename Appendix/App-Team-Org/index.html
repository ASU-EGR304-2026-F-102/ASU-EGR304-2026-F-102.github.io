    Appendix: Team Organization

    Team Goals

    Team 102 established the following shared goals for Project Aurora:

    1. Design and demonstrate a functional system that waters a small garden only when the soil requires water.
    2. Reduce unnecessary water use by using collected rainwater and stopping irrigation when the required soil-moisture level is reached.
    3. Protect the water pump from dry running, blocked tubing, and other unsafe operating conditions.
    4. Design four safe, modular PCBs that communicate reliably and operate together as one complete system.
    5. Give every team member practical experience with circuit design, PCB development, embedded programming, testing, and technical documentation.
    6. Maintain a fair workload, meet project deadlines, and produce professional work that team members can include in their engineering portfolios.

    Main Method for Group Communication

    The team’s primary communication method will be group text messages. Text messages will be used for quick questions, meeting reminders, schedule updates, and urgent information.

    Discord will be used for longer discussions and for organizing links, project files, design resources, and assignment materials. The team will also maintain the required shared Google Drive folder for master copies of documents and other project work. Email will be used for formal communication and instructor correspondence.

    Communication Procedures

    The team will use group text messages for routine communication and Discord for detailed discussions and shared project materials. Team members are expected to respond within a reasonable amount of time and notify the group as early as possible if they cannot complete an assigned task or attend a meeting.

    Sam Kholmuminov will serve as the liaison between the team and the instructor. When the team has a question or issue that requires instructor assistance, the members will explain it to Sam, and Sam will communicate it to the instructor. Sam will also share the instructor’s response with the entire team through the group message and Discord.

    Meeting Coordination

    The team will create a shared calendar containing recurring meetings, assignment deadlines, design reviews, and other important project dates. Calendar notifications and group text messages will be used as meeting reminders.

    If the team needs to add or change a meeting, members will discuss their availability through the group message and select a time that works for everyone. If a team member cannot attend a scheduled meeting, that person must notify the group as early as possible. The team will then decide whether to continue the meeting, reschedule it, or provide the absent member with notes and action items.

    The preferred meeting format is face-to-face because it allows the team to work directly with project hardware and resolve design problems efficiently. Virtual meetings may be used when an in-person meeting is not practical. No additional meeting procedures are currently considered necessary, but the team may revise these procedures if communication problems develop.

    Roles & Responsibilities

    Appendix Table 3: Project Roles and Duties

    Role Duties
    Meeting leader Schedules team meetings, creates and distributes an agenda for each meeting, and runs each meeting
    Meeting recorder Takes minutes of each team meeting, including attendance, and records action items and to whom they are assigned
    Assignment leader Coordinates the team’s work on a given assignment and submits the completed assignment to Canvas before the deadline
    Project monitor Tracks the team’s progress relative to the project schedule and keeps team members informed of deadlines and project status

    The initial roles are assigned as follows:

    • Natalia Castillo-Diaz — Meeting Leader: Schedules meetings, prepares and distributes agendas, and leads each meeting.
    • Arya Padiyar — Meeting Recorder: Records attendance, meeting notes, decisions, action items, responsible members, and completion dates.
    • Taylor Callo — Assignment Leader: Coordinates assignment completion, confirms that the required materials are ready, and submits assignments to Canvas on time.
    • Sam Kholmuminov — Project Monitor and Instructor Liaison: Tracks project progress, maintains the team’s GitHub repository, verifies that website updates are published, monitors deadlines, and communicates with the instructor when necessary.

    Roles will be reviewed after each major assignment or project milestone. The team may rotate roles so that each member gains experience with different responsibilities. Assignments will be based on workload, availability, and the skills each member wants to develop.

    Team members will assist one another when someone has difficulty completing a responsibility. If a role becomes too demanding or a member becomes unavailable, the team will discuss the situation and temporarily redistribute the work. Technical responsibilities for the four project subsystems will be assigned after the system requirements and component options are evaluated.

    Team Coordination & Accountability

    The assignment leader will identify each upcoming deadline and help divide the required work among the team members. Whenever possible, individual contributions should be completed at least 48 hours before the Canvas deadline. The complete assignment will be shared with the team for review at least 24 hours before submission. Each member will review the work and confirm approval through the group message before the assignment leader submits it.

    Sam will verify that the team’s GitHub repository and website contain the most recent approved content. Taylor will verify that the correct links and files are submitted to Canvas. Important project files will be maintained in the shared Google Drive folder, with links organized through Discord.

    Team members will share technical information, explain their subsystem decisions, and help one another develop the skills required for each assignment. If a member needs additional support, the team may arrange a work session, divide the task into smaller steps, consult course resources, or ask the instructor for assistance.

    Feedback from instructors and design reviews will be posted in Discord and discussed during the next team meeting. The team will create action items for each required revision and assign a responsible member and completion date.

    A team member may be considered underperforming if there is a repeated pattern of missed meetings, incomplete assignments, limited communication, or failure to meet agreed-upon deadlines. The team will first contact the member privately, listen to the circumstances, and establish a reasonable improvement plan. If the problem continues, the entire team will discuss the issue and document the missed responsibilities. If no improvement occurs, Sam will bring the documented concern to the instructor and follow the course procedure, which may include a pink slip.

    Conflict Recognition & Resolution

    The team recognizes that disagreements are normal during an engineering project. When a conflict occurs, the members involved will discuss the issue respectfully and focus on project requirements, available evidence, safety, cost, and user needs instead of personal opinions.

    Both sides will be given an opportunity to explain their concerns. The team will attempt to identify common ground and select a solution that best supports the project mission. When possible, competing design ideas will be compared using calculations, testing, or a decision matrix.

    If a member is causing a problem, missing responsibilities, or falling behind, the team will meet with that person privately. The purpose of the meeting will be to understand the situation, identify the required improvement, and agree on specific next steps. If the team cannot resolve the problem internally, Sam, as the instructor liaison, will explain the situation to the instructor and request assistance.
